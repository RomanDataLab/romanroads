Findings

  • Rome's hexagon tops both degree and betweenness — the network data agrees with the proverb.
  • Damascus, Byzantium, Milan, Lepcis Magna and Lyon sit directly on a digitized road (< 0.1 km).
  • Carthage, Jerusalem, Alexandria, Athens and London all connect within 2 km.
  • The unconnected majors cluster in Crete and the Peloponnesian interior — a digitization gap in the DARMC layer, not an ancient reality.
